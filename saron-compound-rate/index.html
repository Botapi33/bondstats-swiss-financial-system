SARON Compound Rate

SARON Compound Rate converts daily SARON observations into a backward-looking interest rate for a defined accrual period.

Definition

SARON Compound Rate converts daily SARON observations into a backward-looking interest rate for a defined accrual period.

Mechanism

The rate originates in the secured CHF money market rather than an unsecured bank panel. Daily observations are aggregated or compounded according to the relevant SIX methodology and contract convention.

Market relevance

Changes in SARON transmit quickly into floating-rate funding, derivatives and SARON-linked mortgages. The spread to the SNB policy rate also reveals conditions in secured franc liquidity.

What to monitor

Watch the SNB policy rate, repo-market conditions, fixing and compounding conventions, reset dates and the contractual margin charged above the reference rate.
