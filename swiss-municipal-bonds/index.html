Swiss Municipal Bonds

Swiss Municipal Bonds belongs to Switzerland’s sub-sovereign funding system, where cantons, municipalities and cantonal banks can carry materially different legal and credit characteristics.

Definition

Swiss Municipal Bonds belongs to Switzerland’s sub-sovereign funding system, where cantons, municipalities and cantonal banks can carry materially different legal and credit characteristics.

Institutional structure

Switzerland’s federal system leaves substantial fiscal autonomy below the Confederation. Debt capacity, tax base, guarantee arrangements and budget rules therefore have to be analysed at the individual issuer level.

Pricing implications

A cantonal bond is not simply a smaller Confederation bond. Liquidity, issue size, fiscal position, investor demand and any legally defined guarantee can create persistent spread differences.

What to monitor

Use the issuer’s accounts, budget rules, debt maturity profile and guarantee legislation. For cantonal banks, distinguish the bank’s own balance sheet from the canton and verify exactly which liabilities a state guarantee covers.
