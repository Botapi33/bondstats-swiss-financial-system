Swiss Post-Trade Infrastructure

Swiss Post-Trade Infrastructure is a piece of Switzerland’s financial-market infrastructure: the plumbing that moves cash, securities or clearing obligations after a trade or payment is initiated.

Definition

Swiss Post-Trade Infrastructure is a piece of Switzerland’s financial-market infrastructure: the plumbing that moves cash, securities or clearing obligations after a trade or payment is initiated.

Transaction path

The relevant distinction is between payment, clearing, securities custody and final settlement. A transaction can pass through several infrastructures, with cash and securities legs subject to different operating and risk controls.

Why it matters

Infrastructure determines when a payment becomes final, how counterparty exposures are reduced and what happens when a participant fails. During stress, timing and collateral rules can matter as much as market prices.

What to monitor

Focus on settlement finality, operating hours, eligible participants, collateral arrangements, default procedures and dependencies between payment, clearing and securities-settlement systems.
